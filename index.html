Published research · Findings of EMNLP 2026

State-Conditioned Steering for LLM Reasoning

  • Developed an inference-time method that recomputes additive activation updates from the model's evolving hidden states during multi-step reasoning, while keeping the backbone model unchanged.
  • Compared state-conditioned updates with fixed-direction steering and prompting-only decoding using paired evaluations and reasoning benchmarks.
  • Evaluated three instruction-tuned backbone models across reasoning benchmarks; the published paper reports larger average gains than prompting-only decoding and baseline activation steering methods.
